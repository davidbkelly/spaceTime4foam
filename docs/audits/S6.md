## Result

The guide covers all 12 required sections and the developer's S5 review
points. Its numerical tables come from the committed result generators:
`makeTables` for orders, norms, seed summaries and cost;
`docs/guideRatios` for the matched-unknown ratios; and the published
Table 2 values stored in `tests/tufillaroTable2/paperTable2.dat` for the
external comparison. The references and claims about the VCFV flux and
geometry were checked against the local papers and the S3/S4 audits.

The guide distinguishes discretisation accuracy from solver cost, gives
the complete CC-1/VC-1 order sequences, qualifies the 6x/9x ratios as
L2 snapshots, and explains the Right-family face-centre/centroid
difference. It explicitly names the S5 audit caveats: interior-only
least-squares exactness, the perturbed boundary strip, the EX corner
fallback, CC-2-LS-EX probe instability and the limits of seed spreads.
