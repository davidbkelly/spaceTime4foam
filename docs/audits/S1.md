## Minor

Status of the first audit's Minor findings:

1. **Point precision.** Fixed in `e7d23f1` (`writePrecision 17`).
   - N = 8 is exact.
   - N = 3 and N = 10 now differ from i/N by at most 1.11e-16. This is
     OpenCASCADE round-off, and the corrected comment in the geo file
     says so.
2. **The meshImport test did not check z, pairing or i/N.** Fixed in
   `43f7bdd`. Faults planted in scratch copies (no tracked files touched):
   - thickness 2 gives FAIL on "distinct z values" and on "front/back
     pairs" (`81 81`); `Alltest` exits 1;
   - a front point at x = 0.5 shifted by 1e-14 gives FAIL on "not
     exactly i/N" (`1`); `Alltest` exits 1;
   - the same shift applied to both the front and back point gives FAIL
     on i/N (`2`); `Alltest` exits 1.
3. **`.gitignore` hid test directories starting with a digit.** Fixed in
   `57e3e42`. See the verification section for what is still ignored.
4. **`gmshToFoam` warns "undefined faces ... defaultFaces".** No change
   is needed. The warning is harmless: the final `boundary` has no
   `defaultFaces` patch, and the test checks for this.
5. **`Allwmake` wording.** Fixed in `9835dd6`: it now prints
   "foam-extend support is untested!".
6. **`docs/repo.md` fails markdownlint.** Still open: MD013 at 18:81 and
   MD009 at 33:79. This is the developer's file.
7. **Padway citation.** Still open. The venue and year in
   `refs/README.md` ("AIAA SciTech 2022 Forum") come from `CLAUDE.md`;
   the PDF itself prints neither. The PDF prints no AIAA paper number, so
   none is given, which is correct. The Tufillaro citation matches the PDF
   (authors, dates, "0:1-22", doi `10.1002/fld.70084`).

New Minor findings from the re-audit:

1. **The pairing check compares only about 6 significant digits.**
   - Location: `tests/meshImport/Allrun`, in the `pointStats` awk block,
     `key = (c[1] + 0) " " (c[2] + 0)`.
   - Awk turns numbers into strings with `CONVFMT="%.6g"`.
   - Evidence: a front point shifted by 1e-14, with its back partner
     unshifted, still passes "front/back pairs" (`81 0`). Only the i/N
     check catches it.
   - At N = 8 the i/N check makes the overall test sound, but the pairing
     check on its own is weaker than its comment claims.
   - Fix: build the key from the raw fields, or use
     `sprintf("%.17g %.17g", ...)`.
2. **Some time-directory names are not ignored.** Time directories with 8
   or more digits (for example `12345678`) or of the form `100.5` are not
   ignored. This is unlikely to matter for iteration counts; widen the
   patterns if needed.
